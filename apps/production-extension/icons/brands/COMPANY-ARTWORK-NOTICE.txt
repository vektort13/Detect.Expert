Detect.Expert local company artwork

The icons are packaged locally for identification of the company or the
explicitly named declaration author already recorded by the catalogue.
The runtime does not download logos and does not infer companies from arbitrary
domains or cookie-name hints.

Original Font Awesome Free 6.7.2 SVGs and their CC-BY-4.0 attribution remain in
PROVENANCE.json and FONT-AWESOME-LICENSE.txt.

Additional Simple Icons are pinned to the exact commits listed in
COMPANY-PROVENANCE.json. Their distributed licence texts are included as
SIMPLE-ICONS-*-LICENSE.txt. Per-icon licence information and primary artwork
references are retained in that provenance ledger.

Other artwork comes from the named company's public site, a documented public
brand catalogue or an offline public favicon cache for the verified company
domain. COMPANY-PROVENANCE.json records the source, identity source, original
and final homepage where available, file hash and any bitmap conversion.
Copyright and trademark ownership remains with the respective company.
