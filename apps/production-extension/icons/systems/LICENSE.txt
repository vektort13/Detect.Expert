SYSTEM ICONS — ATTRIBUTION

The source and transformation of every system mark are recorded in PROVENANCE.json.
Company/product marks remain the artwork and trademarks of their respective owners.
The record for each supplied mark identifies its original source and stated licence.

Marks referenced from ../brands retain the existing attribution and licences in that
directory, including the Font Awesome CC BY 4.0 and Simple Icons source records.

Neutral symbols marked "generic" are project-authored interface artwork. They are
stored separately from company marks and do not identify the author of a technology.
